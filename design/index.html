00The loopWhat the product is. Every screen is one stop on this line.
You say
“A lesson we learned shipping faster this quarter”

That is the whole ask. Goal and audience are optional lines. Everything below happens on its own and shows up as one conversation on Home.

Scout
Researcher

Reads the web and your past posts. Comes back with sources, not vibes.

3 sources
Compass
Strategist

Picks one angle and says why, using what Memory knows works for you.

the angle
Quill ×3
Writers

Three drafts on three models at once. A model that fails gets benched.

3 drafts
Lens
Reviewer

Checks each draft against the platform and your voice rules. Leaves notes.

notes
Verdict
Judge

Scores the drafts blind — it never knows which model wrote which.

a winner
Polish
Editor

Fits the winner to each account: length, hashtags, the image if you asked.

final text
You
Approver

Read it, change a line if you like, approve. It posts at the window that works.
